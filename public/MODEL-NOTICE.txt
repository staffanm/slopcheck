slopcheck fine-tunes the original weights as a four-label claim classifier,
keeps 4 of its 12 layers and the part of its vocabulary that legal Swedish
uses, and distils the result from slopcheck's server classifier, itself
fine-tuned from KBLab/megatron-bert-large-swedish-cased-165-zero-shot (KB,
https://huggingface.co/KBLab/megatron-bert-large-swedish-cased-165-zero-shot).
The weights are exported to ONNX with 4-bit matrix weights and embeddings;
activations remain float32. KB does not endorse slopcheck or its legal assessment labels.
